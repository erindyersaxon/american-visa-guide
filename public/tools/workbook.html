Part of Stage 8: Before you leave the UK

Every stage, in one spreadsheet

Master workbook

One spreadsheet for the whole journey: every stage’s tasks, every document, your costs and your shipping inventory. It is rebuilt from this guide every time the guide changes, so it always says exactly what the site says.

Built on Nikki’s Moving to the USA Workbook. Nikki, a brilliant mod on the Track My Visa server going through the US Embassy London process, built it to share with everyone who needs it. Thank you, Nikki.
Download workbook (.xlsx) Copy to Google Sheets Keep your own copy, share it with your partner, and fill it in as you go.

What’s in it

  1. Tasks

    Every stage’s action list and “Before you move on” checks, in order, from the I-130 to arriving in the US. Each has columns for done, the date and your notes, and a link back to its stage.

  2. Documents

    Every document on the master checklist, the stages that need it, and the public charge evidence pack file codes (P01, B07…).

  3. Costs

    Your savings and expenses, with a running balance.

  4. Boxes

    Your shipping inventory: what is in each box, when and where you bought it, its value for customs and its replacement value for insurance. It flags anything you have owned for less than a year, which you declare separately. See shipping your belongings.

  5. About

    The date your copy was made, how to use it, and our thanks to Nikki.

About this workbook: a free community resource, not legal advice. If you downloaded it a while ago, download it again for the latest version.
