myUSCIS Case Data Reader

Your myUSCIS account loads more about your case than the case page shows: which office holds it, an event code for each step, and when the record last changed. Paste that data here to read it in plain words.

Nothing leaves your browser. We never see your receipt number or your case data, and we do not track them in any way. How your data is handled.

Unofficial, and may not work right now

These are internal USCIS addresses, not a public service. USCIS does not explain the fields, and it can change or block them at any time. Since about 22 November 2026, members report they return "Access Denied", even when signed in. Use this out of interest only. Do not base decisions on it. For a status you can rely on, use your myUSCIS case page.

Approved? Check your Documents tab

No code or status in this data proves an approval. On 22 and 23 July 2026, a USCIS system error showed many I-130s as approved, with an H008 code, when they were not. USCIS reversed it days later, and no approval notices were issued. A real approval always puts an approval notice, a PDF, in the Documents tab of your myUSCIS account. Always check there.

1. Open your case data

Sign in to my.uscis.gov first, in this same browser. Then enter your receipt number to get the three links. Your receipt number stays on this page: it only goes into the links, which go straight to USCIS.

    2. Paste what they show

    On each page that opens, select everything (Ctrl+A, or Cmd+A on a Mac) and copy it. Paste it below. You can paste one page or all three, one after another.

    Your case data stays with you

    This reader works entirely inside your browser, on your own device. There is no American Visa Guide server behind it.

    • We never receive your receipt number or case data. Nothing you type or paste is sent to us or to anyone else. Your receipt number only goes into the links, and those go straight to USCIS.
    • Nothing is saved. No account, no database, and nothing kept in your browser’s storage. Press Clear or close the tab and it is gone.
    • Nothing is tracked, even if you accepted cookies. Saying yes to cookies elsewhere on this site does not apply here: this page never loads Microsoft Clarity, the Ko-fi button or any other third-party tool, so nothing records what you type, paste or read.
    • Nothing is shared or pooled. Your case is not added to any statistics, timelines or “community” data, with or without your name. There is nothing to opt out of.
    • It is free. No sign-up, no paid report, no browser extension and no access to your USCIS sign-in.

    Check it yourself. Once this page has loaded, turn off your Wi-Fi or mobile data and press Read: it still works, because it never needed the internet. Or open your browser’s developer tools (F12), choose the Network tab, and watch: pressing Read sends nothing.

    Comparing this with other case data tools

    Other sites offer to read your case data, and some work differently from this one. Before you paste into any tool, check its privacy policy for:

    • whether your data is sent to its server, or read only in your browser
    • whether it keeps a copy, and for how long
    • whether it adds your case to shared statistics by default, and whether part of your receipt number is kept with it
    • whether it records your visit with session-recording tools while you paste
    • whether a browser extension uses your signed-in USCIS session to fetch your data

    None of these apply here. Our privacy policy says the same.

    What this can and cannot tell you

    • The office comes from jurisdiction in the Office and status link. It is the best sign of where your petition is. See which office has your petition.
    • Event names come from the NIEM code list that USCIS codes are drawn from. A code that is not on that list, such as FTA1, is marked as unknown rather than guessed.
    • An H008 code is not an approval on its own. On 22 and 23 July 2026, a USCIS system error added H008 to many I-130s that had not been approved, and it stayed in many members’ data. A real approval always puts an approval notice, a PDF, in the Documents tab of your myUSCIS account. No approval notice means no approval. Check the tab on the myUSCIS website itself: the documents list in this data stays empty even for approved cases.
    • A record change with no new event does not say what changed. It is not a sign of progress on its own.
    • Never post your receipt number. If you share a screenshot of your case data, cover it everywhere it appears.

    Sources: NIEM, benefit document status codes (copied 27 November 2026). The status shown with each code: a member’s post on r/USCIS, ActionCodes from the USCIS (June 2023). Everything else on this page comes from what members have seen in their own case data, not from USCIS.
