Example · I-130, brother or sister (F4)

Sibling evidence example

A finished record set for a fictional brother and sister. Daniel Moore, a US citizen in Arizona, files an I-130 for his sister Hannah Patel in Leicester. They share their father, Robert, but have different mothers. Seven civil records prove every link in the chain, from Daniel to Robert to Hannah.

What makes it work
  • Every link has a record. Both birth certificates name Robert. Because the link runs through a father, Daniel also sends Robert's marriage certificate to each mother, and the divorce that ended the first marriage (8 CFR 204.2(g)(2)(i), (iv)).
  • Full birth certificates only. The short versions leave out the parents, so they cannot prove a shared parent.
  • Every name difference is explained. Hannah's married name differs from her birth certificate, so her marriage certificate goes in (8 CFR 204.2(g)(5)). The mothers' maiden surnames are on the birth certificates, so they need nothing extra.
  • The status proof covers the age rule. Only a US citizen aged 21 or over can file for a sibling (8 CFR 204.2(g)(1)).
  • No photos and no affidavits. Siblings born to married parents prove the relationship with civil records. Photos cannot stand in for a missing certificate.
  • Hannah's husband and daughter are listed in Part 4 of the I-130. They can immigrate with her without their own petitions (8 CFR 204.2(g)(4)).
Is this your case?
  • You share a mother: both birth certificates showing her name are enough, plus a record of any change to her name.
  • You share a father, and either of you was born to unmarried parents: that sibling also needs proof of legitimation before 18, or of a real relationship with the father before 21. See the parent-child example and choose Parent and child in the evidence template.
  • You are related through adoption or a stepparent: add the adoption decree, or the marriage that created the step relationship, for each of you. See the I-130 checklist.
  • F4 petitions wait years for a visa number. Check the Visa Bulletin for your country.

Part 4: Hannah's family

Daniel lists Hannah's husband, Raj Patel, and their daughter, Maya Patel, aged 3, in Part 4 of the I-130. They share Hannah's priority date and apply for their visas with her. Their own civil documents come later, at the National Visa Center stage.
