Relationship evidence example

A finished photo table for a fictional couple: Sam, a US citizen in Ohio, and Priya, who lives in Leeds. Eight rows cover three years, from the night they met to a visit after filing. Drawn faces stand in for real photos.

What makes it work
  • Each row answers four questions in seconds: who, when, where and what happened.
  • The rows span the whole relationship: meeting, visits, engagement, wedding and a visit after filing. Rows 1 to 4 show intent before the marriage.
  • Both families appear, in both countries. The officer sees other people treating the couple as a couple.
  • One to three photos per event. The wedding gets three because it matters most; a day trip gets one or two.
  • Every date matches a passport stamp or boarding pass. Sam's trips to the UK (rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8) and Priya's to the US (rows 3 and 7) match the travel history on their forms.
  • Every person is named, with their relationship to the couple.
