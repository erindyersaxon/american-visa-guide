Example · I-130, unmarried son or daughter (F1)

Unmarried son or daughter example

A finished record set for a fictional mother and her divorced daughter. Helen Martin, a US citizen in Denver, files an I-130 for her daughter, Sophie Clarke, 30, who lives in Nottingham with her son, Noah. Three civil records prove the relationship, Sophie's name and that she is unmarried. Noah goes in Part 4. The chapters match the Parent and child setting in the evidence template.

What makes it work
  • F1 is for the unmarried sons and daughters of US citizens who are 21 or older (INA 203(a)(1)). Sophie is 30, and divorced counts as unmarried.
  • A mother's petition needs only the birth certificate naming her. Helen sends no marriage certificate of her own (8 CFR 204.2(d)(2)(i)).
  • Sophie's marriage and divorce go in together. The marriage certificate explains her surname, and the final order shows the marriage ended, so the officer sees why the form says unmarried.
  • Noah needs no petition of his own. He is listed in Part 4 and shares his mother's priority date (8 CFR 204.2(d)(4)).
  • No photos, no messages and no affidavits. A daughter named on her birth certificate proves the relationship with civil records.
Is this your case?
  • Your son or daughter has never married: leave out the marriage and divorce records. The birth certificate and your proof of citizenship are enough for a mother; a father adds the parents' marriage certificate.
  • They are under 21: file as IR2. The petition becomes F1 on their 21st birthday and keeps its priority date (8 CFR 204.2(i)(2)).
  • They marry after you file: the petition becomes F3 and keeps its priority date (8 CFR 204.2(i)(1)(i)). See the married son or daughter example.
  • You are a permanent resident: file as F2B. If you naturalize, the petition becomes F1 and keeps its priority date (8 CFR 204.2(i)(3)). If F2B moves faster for their country, they can ask in writing to stay in F2B (INA 204(k)).
  • You are the father and were not married to the mother at the birth: see the parent-child example.
  • F1 petitions wait years for a visa number. Check the Visa Bulletin for your country.
