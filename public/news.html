Embassy and visa news

Announcements from US Embassy London and the US government, press coverage, and what applicants are reporting, newest first. Each item links to the original. Where it helps, there is a short note on what it means for a family visa case at London.

Official items come from a US government source. Press items come from news outlets. Community items are what applicants have reported; the embassy has not confirmed them.

Scope note: this list is checked by hand before anything is added. It is not a complete record of every announcement, so always check the embassy’s visa pages and your own emails from the embassy or NVC.
