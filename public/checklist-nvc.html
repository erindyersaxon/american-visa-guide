IRS Tax Transcript. Treat as required for London: NVC accepts a transcript OR a complete return copy, but London has issued 221(g) after interview for a missing transcript even where DQ was already granted. Obtain the transcript from irs.gov/individuals/get-transcript for every sponsor and joint sponsor and upload it before the interview date. See the NVC guide for what to submit if the transcript is not yet available.

New form edition and a credit check: USCIS accepts only the 08/24/26 edition of the I-864, I-864A and I-864EZ from 1 October 2026. Signing it authorizes USCIS and the State Department to obtain credit reports and scores for every sponsor and every I-864A household member. Lift any credit freeze before signing, or be ready to lift it quickly when asked. See what the I-864 does and does not settle.

Petitioner's US domicile: a joint sponsor cannot fix this. A joint sponsor cures insufficient income. It cannot cure the petitioner's domicile. If the petitioner is not domiciled in the US they do not qualify as a sponsor at all, and State Department guidance to consular officers is explicit: “A joint sponsor cannot be accepted and the applicant must be refused pursuant to INA 212(a)(4)” (9 FAM 601.14-7(a)(3)(c)). A petitioner keeping their principal residence outside the US “could not normally claim a U.S. domicile and would be ineligible to submit Form I-864”. NVC does not always ask for domicile evidence. Upload it anyway. See the petitioner's domicile problem.

Petitioner abroad on qualifying US employment? You may already be US-domiciled. A US citizen living abroad temporarily is treated as domiciled in the US if employed by: the US government; a recognised US research institution; a US firm or corporation engaged in whole or in part in developing US foreign trade or commerce, or a subsidiary of one; a public international organisation the US belongs to by treaty or statute; or a US religious denomination as clergy or a missionary (9 FAM 601.14-7(b), INA 319(b)(1)). Check this before assuming you have a problem. An LPR sponsor abroad is similarly domiciled in the US if they obtained the preservation of residence benefit under INA 316(b) or 317.

4. Document categories at NVC stage

Category Required Notes
Civil (Identity) ✓ Birth certificates for all applicants (certified copies). Marriage certificate (certified). If previously married, all divorce decrees or death certificates (certified).
Translation ✓ Every document not in English, or not in the official language of the country where you apply, must come with a certified English translation, stating the translator is competent and the translation accurate.
Financial ✓ Sponsor: IRS tax transcript, employment letter, financial evidence. Joint sponsor: the same requirements, on their own separate I-864. Form I-864A is only for a household member whose income a sponsor is counting. See Section 3 for detailed list.
Forms ✓ DS-260 (one per applicant, submitted in CEAC). I-864 (wet-signed by sponsor, required for all). I-864A only for a household member whose income is being counted (not for a joint sponsor).
Police ✓ Every applicant aged 16 or over needs certificates from: their current country of residence (for the UK, ACRO) if they have lived there more than 6 months; their country of nationality if they lived there more than 6 months; any other country where they lived more than 12 months; and any country where they were arrested. See Section 5.
Supporting * Passport biographic page, military records, adoption documents, name change deed (if applicable). See Section 6.
Medical n/a Medical exam not required at NVC stage. Scheduled for Medical Day closer to interview date.

For a complete breakdown of all document requirements at each stage (I-130, NVC, Medical, Interview) with detailed notes on formats and translations, see Master Document Checklist.

5. Police certificates (ACRO for UK)

ACRO (UK):

  • Standard: £68 / about 30 days
  • Priority (2 working days): suspended. Allow the full standard time
  • Website: acro.police.uk
  • Validity: 12 months for embassy purposes
  • When to order: toward the end of the I-130 wait or early at NVC
