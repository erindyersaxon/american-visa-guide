Stage 3: Waiting for your interview letter

⏱ DQ to IL averaged 77 days in the six months to July 2026 No drop since 14 July 2026 IL to interview ~2 months Reviewed 27 Sept 2026

You are here: You are Documentarily Qualified and in the embassy queue for an interview. CEAC will keep showing “At NVC” until your interview letter arrives.

What happens: No interview letters have been sent since 14 July 2026. Use this time to get organized.

Do this first: Write your public charge statement, and check your tax documents are current.

Action list

  1. Beneficiary: Write the public charge statement with your petitioner, and build the evidence pack behind it. Upload both to your case in CEAC, then email them to LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov.
  2. Petitioner: If the US tax filing deadline has passed since you became DQ, upload the latest tax transcript to CEAC. The steps are in Stage 4.
  3. Beneficiary: Check your ACRO certificate and passport will still be valid. Your passport must last at least 6 months past the date you plan to enter the US.
  4. Beneficiary: Check you have every police certificate you need, in date. That includes any country of nationality you no longer live in but lived in for more than 6 months. Australia’s takes up to two months.
  5. Beneficiary: Arrange health insurance to cover you until you can enroll in the US. Keep the quote or certificate for your statement.
  6. Beneficiary: When the IL arrives, go to Stage 4: Interview letter received.

Once you are DQ, NVC has everything it needs and your case joins the embassy queue for an interview. You wait, and you prepare. CEAC keeps showing "At NVC" until your interview letter arrives.

Wait times

The wait from DQ to interview letter varies and changes over time. See the data page for current community averages.

How IL drops work

The embassy schedules interviews in batches, which members call drops. Drops have no fixed schedule. Until mid-2026 they came at least once a month, sometimes more. Whether a drop includes you depends on your DQ date compared with others in the queue. No batch has been reported since 14 July 2026. See below.

What 16 months of drops tell us

From 25 drops the community tracked, March 2025 – July 2026. Updated 3 September 2026.

  • A monthly rhythm. One main batch every 4 to 5 weeks, with small top-ups between. Each main batch fills about one calendar month of interview slots.
  • Weekday afternoons. See when ILs arrive.
  • Mostly first come, first served. Every third or fourth batch is a wider catch-up that picks up people who have waited longer than average. If you feel skipped, expect one of those to include you.
  • A longer wait from DQ to IL. It averaged 77 days over the six months to July 2026, up from 63 all-time.
  • IL to interview was two calendar months, without exception up to July 2026. The embassy is now cancelling interviews it scheduled that way: see the cancellation notice.

Right now: drops have stopped. The last, small one came on 14 July 2026. The gap since is longer than any in the 16 months tracked, and members at other posts report the same. Read this card as history, not a forecast.

This data reflects Immediate Relative (IR) visas

Nearly all members hold Immediate Relative (IR) visas, so the drop figures describe IR cases. Family-Sponsored Preference (F) cases run about a month behind. On 8 July 2026 the State Department's NVC scheduling status tool showed London scheduling IR cases from April 2026 and F cases from March 2026. Use that tool for official figures. The tracker counts only members who send in their dates.

When do ILs arrive?

From what London members have seen, ILs come by email from National_Visa_Center@state.gov with the subject Immigrant Visa Interview Appointment. They always arrive on a weekday, usually early to mid afternoon UK time (around 13:50–16:00). Friday is most common, then Wednesday and Monday. No drop has ever come at a weekend. CEAC then updates to show your case at the embassy.

The IL Drop History table on the data page keeps every drop date, day and time.

Use the waiting time to prepare

This is your best chance to get organized before things speed up. Check each of these is up to date, and start your binder.

Documents & admin
ACRO police certificate
Valid 12 months for embassy purposes.
Standard: £68, about 30 days  ·  Priority (2 working days): suspended
ACRO has suspended its 2-day priority service, so you cannot pay to rush a late order. Allow the full standard time. If the certificate you uploaded at NVC will be more than 12 months old on your interview date, order a new one by the day your interview letter arrives.
acro.police.uk →
Other police certificates
Check you have every certificate you need, and each is still in date for your interview. That includes any country of nationality you no longer live in but lived in for more than 6 months, even if that was as a child. People miss these most, and they are usually the slowest to get from the UK. Not sure which you need? Use the police certificate decision tree in Stage 2.
Australia (AFP National Police Check): allow two months. The AFP accepts only ink fingerprints on paper, with full palm and side prints, and few UK providers still take them. Phone the AFP to confirm the form. Tracked post shows nothing until the envelope reaches Australia. The fee leaving your account tells you it arrived. Members reported just under a month, and 57 days, start to finish.
afp.gov.au: National Police Checks →
Civil document originals
Check you can lay your hands on the originals: marriage certificate, birth certificate, divorce decrees. Check nothing expires or needs replacing.
I-864 financial documents
Recent pay stubs · Employment letter confirming salary · IRS tax transcripts (up to 3 years) · W-2s. A joint sponsor needs the same documents.
If a sponsor is self-employed: 12 months of up-to-date business bank statements and a profit and loss statement for this year so far. The tax returns you filed at NVC may now be over a year old.
Passport expiry
Your passport must last at least 6 months past the date you plan to enter the US. Renew it now if needed.

Medical preparation: check your vaccination records against the CDC list now, and gather reports for any significant condition. Request the GP Summary Care Record later, 2 to 3 weeks before the medical. Stage 5 covers each.

Use this prep time to head off a 221(g)

London cases most often stall at the window over a money gap you could have fixed while waiting: a missing IRS transcript, income near the threshold with no joint sponsor, or out-of-date tax documents. Our 221(g) page lists what to put in place now, before your interview.

How to prevent a 221(g) →

Public charge: prepare your written statement

The officer now tests public charge in every case. A sufficient I-864 no longer settles it. The officer weighs five factors about you, the beneficiary: age, health, family status, assets and resources, and education and skills. You carry the burden of proof. Use the waiting time to get this done:

  • Write the statement. The statement builder takes about 30 minutes. Answer "how will this be paid for" with recurring income in every section, and put savings last as a buffer. London does not let assets stand in for income.
  • Gather the evidence. The last 3 years of IRS tax returns for every sponsor, the last 3 months of payslips and bank statements, proof of the petitioner's health insurance, and your UK student loan statement if you have one.
  • Send it at least five working days before the interview. Upload it to CEAC first, then email LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov so the unit knows it is there. Bring a printed copy.
  • Rehearse the answers out loud. The officer asks you, not your sponsor. Learn the petitioner's income, debts and insurance as well as your own.

Health insurance: the coverage gap

Plan for two periods:

Before you arrive. You usually cannot get US health insurance until you live in the US and can enroll. Short-term international health insurance can cover the gap. Members have used:

  • Cigna Global: gives an online quote in a few minutes at cignaglobal.com. You do not have to buy. You can download the quote summary as a PDF and add it to your public charge statement, to show you have researched coverage.
  • IMG Patriot America Plus: members have bought 6 months of coverage without a US SSN. IMG issues a certificate of coverage you can add to your public charge statement.

These are examples from members, not recommendations. Compare plans to find one that suits you.

After you arrive. Once you are a permanent resident living in the US, you can enroll through the ACA marketplace at healthcare.gov. Plans come at a range of prices. Premium tax credits exist for lower incomes, but the enhanced credits expired at the end of 2025, and the 2025 budget law narrowed who among immigrants qualifies. Permanent residents still qualify. Price the premium without any subsidy. In your public charge statement, show you will pay for coverage from income, not a subsidy. You can browse plans before you arrive.

What to prepare and when

Get your quote or certificate of coverage before your medical. Bring a printed or digital copy to the medical and the interview. If you send a public charge statement to LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov before your interview, include it there too. You do not have to buy a policy. A quote that shows you have researched and budgeted for coverage is enough. One exception: if you have an ongoing condition that will come up at your medical, a policy you have bought answers "who pays for that care" far better than a quote.

Common questions

Can the petitioner come to the medical or the interview? The answers differ. Not the embassy: the embassy does not let petitioners in for the document check or the interview. The beneficiary goes alone. The only exceptions are an interpreter, or one companion for a visitor with special needs. The medical is more flexible: VisaMedicals lets a companion wait, which helps if you need someone to mind children. Pre-arrange it with the clinic by email, about a week ahead. Be warned: the waiting area is small and gets very hot, and the building has steps, so a pram needs help.

Do I need the original I-864 mailed to me? No. A printed copy is fine.

Do I need two separate trips to London? Yes, unless you have an expedited interview: one trip for the medical and document check, one for the interview. Distance and cost do not change this. Book travel and somewhere to stay well in advance.

Expedited interviews

The embassy offers expedited interviews for real emergencies, such as serious medical need. They are not a way to jump the queue. Community data shows 4 expedited cases. We leave them out of wait time figures, because they do not reflect the normal process.

To ask for one, email NVCexpedite@state.gov
