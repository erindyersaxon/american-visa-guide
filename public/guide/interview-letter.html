Stage 4: Interview letter received

⏱ IL to interview ~2 months Medical at least 10 working days before your interview Reviewed 14 Oct 2026

Bottom line

You are here: Your interview letter has arrived from National_Visa_Center@state.gov. It gives your interview date, usually two calendar months away.

What happens: Medical appointment windows open once a month and fill fast. If your interview falls after the US tax filing deadline, you need to update your tax documents in CEAC.

Do this first: Book your medical with VisaMedicals. Then register your interview on AIS and choose how your passport comes back to you.

Action list

  1. Beneficiary: Book your medical with VisaMedicals, at least 10 working days before your interview date.
  2. Beneficiary: Register your interview on AIS using the first option, and choose your passport return method.
  3. Beneficiary: If the ACRO certificate you uploaded at NVC will be more than 12 months old on your interview date, order a new one today. See Stage 3.
  4. Petitioner: If your medical and interview fall after the US tax filing deadline, upload the most recent tax transcript to CEAC. See Check your financial documents are current for the steps.
  5. Beneficiary: Put your interview date into the interview date checker to get every deadline that follows from it.

Your interview date is usually two calendar months after the IL arrives. See the observed IL to interview pattern in Stage 3.

What the IL email looks like

The Interview Letter comes from National_Visa_Center@state.gov: see When do ILs arrive? for timing. An additional email may come from the embassy at LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov. It looks like this:

From: London, LNDIVSubmissions <LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov>
To: [Beneficiary] / [Petitioner]
Subject: U.S. Immigrant visa application – LND01234567891

Immigrant Visa Unit · Embassy of the United States of America · 33 Nine Elms Lane, London, SW11 7US

Dear [Beneficiary],

Case Number: LND01234567891

Interview: [TIME] [DAY], [DATE]

We are pleased to confirm that the Immigrant Visa Unit at the U.S. Embassy in London has received an approved petition in your name. To complete your application, you must attend two separate appointments at the U.S. Embassy:

  • Document Review – Immediately following your medical examination
  • Visa Interview – With an immigration consular officer

[Email continues with steps 1–4 and public charge statement. See Stage 5: Medical exam and Stage 6: Embassy interview]

Actions after receiving your IL

Beneficiary: Two things to action as soon as you can

1. Book your medical via VisaMedicals: appointment windows open monthly and fill quickly. The medical must be 10 working days before your interview date. You are required to attend the same-day embassy document check afterwards (window: 11:45 AM – 2:30 PM).

2. Register your interview via AIS. You must select your passport return method at this stage: home delivery or Mail Boxes Etc collection (Holborn, Angel, or Belfast). If you do not complete this, the embassy has no way to return your passport to you. Note: registration may take a few days to appear after the IL arrives.

Which option to pick in AIS. AIS asks you to "select the condition that applies to you". If your IL already gives an interview date, choose the first option: "I have received a Consular interview appointment date from the National Visa Center (NVC), Kentucky Consular Center (KCC), or the Consular Section and I have been instructed to register my appointment on this site." The embassy has already scheduled your interview, so you only need to register it. Do not choose the immigrant visa option that says "schedule". Members have shared that they chose the first option, entered the beneficiary's details, and AIS pulled up the interview date and time that matched their IL.

Registration on AIS may take a few days after the IL arrives. If you have previously used AIS for a non-immigrant visa (e.g. B1/B2), you will likely need to create a new account for the immigrant visa process. Escalate after ~5 business days if unresolved: UK.Visas@gdit-gss.com

Check your financial documents are current

If you were DQ before the current tax year filing deadline, and your medical appointment and interview are scheduled after the tax filing deadline (typically April 15 in the US), your financials may be out of date. Consular officers need to review the most recent tax year to make a public charge assessment.

What to do: Unless you have filed a filing extension (in which case you can upload an explanation), update your tax documents in CEAC so that consular officers can review the latest tax year.

Petitioner: Step-by-step: Upload updated tax documents
  1. Get your transcript: Visit the IRS Get Transcript portal to download your Tax Return Transcript for the most recent tax year. You will need to create or log into an ID.me account for secure access.
  2. Format your files: Save your tax documents as PDF or JPEG files. Each file must be smaller than 2 MB.
  3. Log into CEAC: Go to the CEAC Login Page using your Case Number and Invoice ID.
  4. Upload: Navigate to the Affidavit of Support section and upload the new tax transcript, along with any relevant W-2s or Schedules, as supplemental or updated financial evidence.

Getting an ID.me account

The IRS now requires all users to verify identity through ID.me. This is a secure identity verification service used by multiple US government agencies.

Petitioner: Create your ID.me account
  1. Visit id.me and click "Sign up"
  2. Choose to verify with your IRS account or use your driver's license, state ID, or passport
  3. Follow the identity verification prompts. You may need to take a selfie and photos of your ID
  4. Once verified, you will have instant access to the IRS Get Transcript tool
  5. Download your Tax Return Transcript and upload it to CEAC as described above
