Stage 4: Interview letter received

⏱ Interview about 2 months after the letter Medical at least 10 working days before your interview Reviewed 14 Oct 2026

You are here: Your interview letter has arrived from National_Visa_Center@state.gov. It gives your interview date, usually two calendar months away.

What happens: Medical appointment windows open once a month and fill fast. If your interview falls after the US tax filing deadline, you need to update your tax documents in CEAC.

Do this first: Book your medical with VisaMedicals. Then register your interview on AIS and choose how your passport comes back to you.

Action list

  1. Beneficiary: Book your medical with VisaMedicals, at least 10 working days before your interview date.
  2. Beneficiary: Register your interview on AIS, using the first option. Choose how your passport comes back to you.
  3. Beneficiary: Will the ACRO certificate you uploaded at NVC be more than 12 months old on your interview date? If so, order a new one today. See Stage 3.
  4. Petitioner: If your medical and interview fall after the US tax filing deadline, upload the latest tax transcript to CEAC. The steps are under Check your financial documents are current.
  5. Beneficiary: Enter your interview date in the interview date checker. It works out every deadline that follows.

  • Interview date checker: the public charge, medical, passport and ACRO deadlines that follow from your date

Your interview is usually two calendar months after the interview letter (IL) arrives. Stage 3 shows the pattern members have seen.

What the IL email looks like

The interview letter comes from National_Visa_Center@state.gov. For timing, see When do ILs arrive? The embassy may also email you from LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov. That email looks like this:

From: London, LNDIVSubmissions <LNDIVSubmissions@state.gov>
To: [Beneficiary] / [Petitioner]
Subject: U.S. Immigrant visa application – LND01234567891

Immigrant Visa Unit · Embassy of the United States of America · 33 Nine Elms Lane, London, SW11 7US

Dear [Beneficiary],

Case Number: LND01234567891

Interview: [TIME] [DAY], [DATE]

We are pleased to confirm that the Immigrant Visa Unit at the U.S. Embassy in London has received an approved petition in your name. To complete your application, you must attend two separate appointments at the U.S. Embassy:

  • Document Review – Immediately following your medical examination
  • Visa Interview – With an immigration consular officer

[Email continues with steps 1–4 and public charge statement. See Stage 5: Medical exam and Stage 6: Embassy interview]

What to do once your letter arrives

Beneficiary: Two things to do as soon as you can

1. Book your medical with VisaMedicals. Appointment windows open once a month and fill fast. The medical must be at least 10 working days before your interview. Afterwards, the same day, you must go to the embassy document check (window: 11:45 AM – 2:30 PM).

2. Register your interview on AIS. Here you choose how your passport comes back: home delivery, or collection from Mail Boxes Etc in Holborn, Angel or Belfast. Without this step, the embassy has no way to return your passport. Registration can take a few days to appear after the IL arrives.

Which option to pick in AIS. AIS asks you to "select the condition that applies to you". If your IL already gives an interview date, choose the first option: "I have received a Consular interview appointment date from the National Visa Center (NVC), Kentucky Consular Center (KCC), or the Consular Section and I have been instructed to register my appointment on this site." The embassy has already scheduled your interview, so you only need to register it. Do not choose the immigrant visa option that says "schedule". Members who chose the first option entered the beneficiary's details, and AIS showed the interview date and time from their IL.

If you used AIS before for a non-immigrant visa, such as a B1/B2, you will probably need a new account for the immigrant visa. If registration still has not worked after about 5 business days, email UK.Visas@gdit-gss.com.

Check your financial documents are current

Your financial documents may be out of date if both of these are true:

  • you became DQ before this year's US tax filing deadline (usually April 15)
  • your medical and interview fall after that deadline

The consular officer needs the most recent tax year to make the public charge assessment.

What to do: update your tax documents in CEAC so the officer can see the latest tax year. If you filed for an extension, upload an explanation instead.

Petitioner: Upload updated tax documents, step by step
  1. Get your transcript: download your Tax Return Transcript for the latest tax year from the IRS Get Transcript portal. You sign in with an ID.me account.
  2. Prepare your files: save each document as a PDF or JPEG under 2 MB.
  3. Sign in to CEAC: use your Case Number and Invoice ID on the CEAC login page.
  4. Upload: in the Affidavit of Support section, upload the new transcript and any W-2s or Schedules, as updated financial evidence.

Getting an ID.me account

The IRS now makes every user prove their identity through ID.me, a service several US government agencies use.

Petitioner: Create your ID.me account
  1. Go to id.me and select "Sign up"
  2. Verify with your IRS account, or with your driver's license, state ID or passport
  3. Follow the prompts. You may need to take a selfie and photos of your ID
  4. Once ID.me verifies you, you can use the IRS Get Transcript tool
  5. Download your Tax Return Transcript and upload it to CEAC, as above
