Stage 7: After the interview

⏱ Passport: ~4 days by pick-up, ~11 days by delivery (community averages) $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee Reviewed 27 Sept 2026

Bottom line

You are here: You have had your interview.

What happens: Most London outcomes recorded by the community are approvals. The embassy keeps your passport to print the visa and returns it by courier. Community averages: 4 days after the interview for pick-up, 11 days for home delivery. A 221(g) pauses your case until you send exactly what the ticked boxes ask for.

Do this first: Add the courier emails to your safe senders, and pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee.

Action list

  1. Beneficiary: Your passport comes back by courier, or you collect it from Mail Boxes Etc. Add donotreply@usvisa-info.com and noreply@mbeglobal.com to your safe senders now.
  2. Beneficiary: Pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee at my.uscis.gov. We recommend paying before you travel.
  3. Beneficiary: Plan your travel. You must enter the US within 6 months of your medical exam, because your medical results last 6 months. Work out your last entry date.
  4. Beneficiary: Track the case at ceac.state.gov with your LND case number and passport number.
  5. Either: Set up myUSCIS accounts: one for the beneficiary, and one each for the petitioner and any joint sponsor, linked to the case. Why.
  6. Beneficiary: Handed a 221(g)? Send exactly what the ticked boxes ask for. The 221(g) guide shows how.

Possible outcomes

Approved

The embassy keeps your passport to print the visa. You do not need to send anything else. In community data, 94% of recorded London outcomes are approvals at interview. Members chose to report these themselves, mostly before the public charge worksheet began. Treat the figure as a rough guide, not an official approval rate.

221(g): refused until you send more

The officer pauses your case until you send more. You get a green Consular Section: Immigrant Visa Unit sheet at the window, with boxes ticked. The most common cause at London is financial sponsorship: a missing IRS transcript, for example, or income judged too low without a joint sponsor. The ticked boxes are all you need to clear. Send exactly what they ask for and the case moves again. Send it only the way the embassy's follow-up email tells you to. See if you already have a 221(g).

You can prevent almost every London 221(g). Our 221(g) page shows the green sheet, what to have in place before interview day, and how to clear one if you get it, including the one-year §203(g) deadline.

Full guide: how to prevent and clear a 221(g) →
Denied

The consular officer explains the refusal and whether you have any options. If you qualify, you may file Form I-601 (Application for Waiver of Grounds of Inadmissibility). You must send Form I-601 to a US address, even if you are outside the United States. The I-601 flowchart shows where and how to file.

Tracking your case in CEAC

Track your case at ceac.state.gov with your LND case number and passport number. What each status means:

  • Ready: the case is at the embassy or waiting for final processing
  • Administrative Processing: normal during final checks and printing after approval. A short spell of this after a successful interview is routine. It is not a 221(g).
  • Issued: the visa is approved and printed. The courier gets your passport next.

Do not upload 221(g) evidence to CEAC unless the embassy's email tells you to, because nobody will read it. Members who uploaded documents after the interview without that instruction saw them stay at Submitted for good.

Surname with an apostrophe? Use a space

Members report that the status tracker can fail to find a case when the surname has an apostrophe. Type a space in its place: O Shea for O'Shea.

Getting your passport back

AIS tracking mostly stays at "Appointment" and does not show where your passport is. The best signal is the email that says the embassy has released your passport to the courier.

Add these to your safe senders now

donotreply@usvisa-info.com, subject: "Yatri: U.S. Department of State Visa Documents Ready for Pickup". It confirms the embassy has released your passport to the courier.

noreply@mbeglobal.com: home delivery notification from Mail Boxes Etc.

Beneficiary: Collection from Mail Boxes Etc

Usually 2–3 working days after the interview: a Thursday interview means a Monday or Tuesday collection. Community average: 4 days.

You get an email when your documents are ready. Bring ID (see the list below) and your tracking number. If you do not collect within 30 days, your passport goes back to the embassy.

Holborn: 19 Bury Place WC1A 2JB, Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00
Angel: 8 Duncan Street N1 8BW
Belfast: 38 Montgomery Business Park BT6 9HL, Mon–Fri 08:30–16:30
Visa queries: 0208 064 1342

Beneficiary: Home or office delivery

Usually 5–7 days after the interview, and up to 2 weeks depending on the route. Community average: 11 days.

The courier tries up to 3 times at your address. After that, the package goes back to the embassy. At a home address, the courier hands it only to an adult who signs for it. At a business address, staff who can sign for packages can take it.

If the first try fails, the courier leaves a card. On the next tries, you must show ID at the door (see the list below). Watch for email from noreply@mbeglobal.com. Weekends and UK bank holidays can delay delivery, so a late-week interview often means delivery the next week.

ID for collection or redelivery

To collect your own documents, or to accept a redelivery at home, show any one of these:

  • Official or state-issued photo ID card
  • Official photo driving licence
  • Birth certificate
  • Debit or credit card
  • Two utility bills
Beneficiary: Parents collecting for children

To collect for your child, prove you are the parent with:

  • A copy of the child's birth certificate or adoption decree that shows at least one parent's full legal name
  • Your own ID from the list above, in the same name as on the child's birth certificate or adoption decree
Beneficiary: Sending someone else

An adult applicant can authorize someone else to collect the passport, or to accept a redelivery. That person must bring:

  • A letter of authorization signed by you, with their full legal name
  • A photocopy of one of your ID documents from the list above
  • Their own ID from the list above, in the same name as the letter

USCIS Immigrant Fee ($235)

USCIS produces your green card (Form I-551) only after you pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee. [USCIS: Immigrant Fee] Pay before you travel if you can. USCIS aims to deliver the card within 90 days. It counts from your entry date if you paid before you entered, or from your payment date if you paid after.

Pay this fee as soon as possible

If you do not pay, you get no physical green card. You can use the visa stamp in your passport until the card arrives. Many employers and organizations do not know immigration documents, though, and will question it.

Pay at my.uscis.gov by credit or debit card, or from a US bank account (ACH). You do not need to sign in. You need two numbers from your visa stamp:

  • A-Number: the Registration Number. If it has fewer than 9 digits, the site adds a leading zero.
  • DOS Case ID: the IV Case Number without its last two digits (e.g. "ABC1234567801" → enter "ABC12345678").
Payment page will not load? Try mobile data

Members paying from the UK report that the payment page can fail to load, or show an error, on home Wi-Fi. Turning Wi-Fi off and paying over mobile data worked.

A-Number not recognised at "Submit and Pay"? Retry

Members report that the form can save an entry, then reject the A-Number at the final "Submit and Pay" step. First check the number against the Registration Number on your visa stamp. If it matches, keep going. Members got through by filling in the form again from the bottom field up, or by pressing the button again and again until the site accepted the A-Number.

Keep the receipt. Its IOE number lets you track the card. Stage 9 covers tracking the green card and getting your Social Security card after you arrive.
