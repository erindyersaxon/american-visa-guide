Stage 7: After the interview

⏱ Passport: ~4 days by pick-up, ~11 days by delivery (community averages) $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee Reviewed 27 Sept 2026

Bottom line

You are here: You have had your interview.

What happens: Most London outcomes recorded by the community are approvals. The embassy keeps your passport to print the visa and returns it by courier. Community averages: 4 days after the interview for pick-up, 11 days for home delivery. A 221(g) pauses your case until you send exactly what the ticked boxes ask for.

Do this first: Add the courier emails to your safe senders, and pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee.

Action list

  1. Beneficiary: Your passport comes back by courier delivery or for collection from Mail Boxes Etc: add donotreply@usvisa-info.com and noreply@mbeglobal.com to your safe senders now.
  2. Beneficiary: Pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee at my.uscis.gov. Paying before you travel is recommended.
  3. Beneficiary: Plan your travel: you must enter the US within 6 months of your medical exam, the period your medical results are valid for. Work out your last entry date.
  4. Beneficiary: Track the case at ceac.state.gov with your LND case number and passport number.
  5. Either: Set up myUSCIS accounts: the beneficiary’s own, and the petitioner’s and any joint sponsor’s linked to the case. Why.
  6. Beneficiary: Handed a 221(g)? Supply exactly what the ticked boxes ask for, using the 221(g) guide.

Possible outcomes

Approved

Passport retained for visa printing. No further documents required. Community data: 94% of recorded outcomes at London are approvals at interview. These are self-reported by members who chose to share, mostly from before the public charge worksheet, so treat the figure as indicative, not as an official approval rate.

221(g): Administrative Processing

Case paused pending additional items, issued at the window on a green Consular Section: Immigrant Visa Unit sheet with the applicable boxes ticked. Most common cause at London: financial sponsorship issues, such as a missing IRS transcript or income judged too low without a joint sponsor. The ticked boxes are the entire bar to clear; supply exactly what is asked and the case resumes. Send it the way the embassy's follow-up email instructs, and by no other route. See if you already have a 221(g).

Almost every London 221(g) is preventable. Our dedicated page reproduces the green sheet, sets out the mitigations to have in place before interview day, and covers how to resolve one if you get it, including the one-year §203(g) deadline.

Full guide: how to prevent and resolve a 221(g) →
Denied

The consular officer will explain the refusal and whether recourse is available. If eligible, you may file Form I-601 (Application for Waiver of Grounds of Inadmissibility). Form I-601 must be sent to a US address even if you are outside the United States. See the I-601 flowchart for filing guidance.

CEAC status tracking

Track at ceac.state.gov using your LND case number and passport number.

  • Ready: case at embassy or pending final processing
  • Administrative Processing: normal during final checks and printing after approval. Short-term AP after a successful interview is routine and is not the same as a 221(g).
  • Issued: visa approved and printed. Passport will be handed to courier next.

Uploading 221(g) evidence to CEAC is not a way to get it read unless the embassy's email tells you to. Documents uploaded there after the interview without that instruction have been reported to stay at Submitted indefinitely.

Surname with an apostrophe? Use a space

Community members report that the status tracker can fail to find a case when the surname contains an apostrophe. Enter the surname with a space in its place: O Shea for O'Shea.

Getting your passport back

AIS tracking mostly stays at "Appointment" and doesn't reflect where the passport is. The most reliable signal is the email notification when the passport is released to courier.

Add these to your safe senders now

donotreply@usvisa-info.com, subject: "Yatri: U.S. Department of State Visa Documents Ready for Pickup". This confirms your passport has been released to the courier.

noreply@mbeglobal.com: home delivery notification from Mail Boxes Etc.

Beneficiary: Pick Up (Mail Boxes Etc)

Typically 2–3 working days after interview. Example: Thursday interview → Monday/Tuesday pickup. Community average: 4 days.

You'll be notified by email when your documents are ready for pick-up. Bring proof of identity (see ID requirements below) and your tracking number. Passports not retrieved within 30 days are returned to the originating Consular Section.

Holborn: 19 Bury Place WC1A 2JB, Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00
Angel: 8 Duncan Street N1 8BW
Belfast: 38 Montgomery Business Park BT6 9HL, Mon–Fri 08:30–16:30
Visa queries: 0208 064 1342

Beneficiary: Home or Office Delivery

Typically 5–7 days after interview, up to 2 weeks depending on routing. Community average: 11 days.

The courier will attempt delivery up to 3 times at the requested address; if unsuccessful, the package is returned to the originating consular section. At a home address the parcel is released only to an adult willing to sign for it; at a business address it is released to personnel authorized to sign for packages.

If the first attempt fails, the courier leaves a calling card. For redelivery attempts, the applicant must prove their identity at the time of delivery (see ID requirements below). Watch for email from noreply@mbeglobal.com. Weekends and UK bank holidays may delay delivery: late-week interviews often shift into the following week.

ID for collection or redelivery

Applicants retrieving their own documents at a pick-up location, or receiving a redelivery attempt at home: must prove their identity with any one of the following:

  • Official or State issued identification card (with photo)
  • Official driver's license (with photo)
  • Birth Certificate
  • Debit or Credit Card
  • Two Utility Bills
Beneficiary: Parents collecting for children

Parents retrieving documents for their children must prove the relationship by presenting:

  • A copy of the child's birth certificate or adoption decree, with at least one of the parent's full legal names displayed on the document
  • The parent's own identity, proven with one of the documents in the list above, with a name matching the child's birth certificate or adoption decree
Beneficiary: Authorizing a third party

Adult applicants may authorize a third party to retrieve their passport at a pick-up location, or to accept a redelivery on their behalf. The third party must present:

  • An applicant-signed letter of authorization containing the third party's full legal name
  • A photocopy of one of the applicant's identification documents from the list above
  • The third party's own ID from the list above. The name must match the authorization letter

USCIS Immigrant Fee ($235)

USCIS produces your green card (Form I-551) only after you pay the $235 USCIS Immigrant Fee. [USCIS: Immigrant Fee] Pay before you travel if you can. USCIS counts the card's 90-day delivery window from your entry date if you paid before entry, or from your payment date if you paid after.

Pay this fee as soon as possible

If you do not pay, you get no physical green card. You can use the visa stamp in your passport until the card arrives, but many employers and institutions unfamiliar with immigration status will question it.

Pay at my.uscis.gov by credit/debit card or US bank account (ACH). You do not need to log in. You will need two numbers from your visa stamp:

  • A-Number: the Registration Number. If it has fewer than 9 digits, the site adds a leading zero.
  • DOS Case ID: the IV Case Number without its last two digits (e.g. "ABC1234567801" → enter "ABC12345678").
Payment page will not load? Try mobile data

Community members paying from the UK report that the payment page can fail to load or return an error on home Wi-Fi. Turning Wi-Fi off and paying over mobile data has got the payment through.

A-Number not recognised at "Submit and Pay"? Retry

Community members report that the form can save an entry but then reject the A-Number at the final "Submit and Pay" step. Check the number against the Registration Number on your visa stamp first. If it matches, keep going: members got the payment through by refilling the form from the bottom field up instead of top down, or by rage-pressing the button until the site gave in and accepted the A-Number.

Keep the receipt: its IOE number tracks the card. Stage 9 covers tracking the green card and getting your Social Security card after you arrive.

Before you move on

  • Beneficiary: My passport with the visa is back, collected or delivered.
  • Beneficiary: The Immigrant Fee is paid, and I have the I-551 IOE receipt number to track the green card.
  • Either: The beneficiary, petitioner and any joint sponsor each have a myUSCIS account.
  • Beneficiary: If someone else is collecting my passport, they have my signed authorization letter, a copy of my ID and their own ID.
  • Beneficiary: If I was issued a 221(g), I have supplied exactly what was ticked.
