Interview Day Checklist

Before August 2026 most reported London interviews took under 5 minutes; since the public charge worksheet, expect 20–40 minutes. Preparation still makes the difference.

Note: Petitioners and attorneys are not permitted in the waiting room or at the interview. You attend alone, except for an interpreter or one accompanying person for special needs visitors.

3. On the day

  • Skip the main queue. Go to the desk at the front and tell staff "I have an immigrant visa interview." Arrive 15 to 30 minutes early.
  • Read the sticker on the back of your passport. Staff may not mention it. It shows your queue number and booth; check the name and case number too. If you are not sure you are in the right waiting area, show it to staff and ask.
  • Bring originals and copies of your civil documents, such as birth and marriage certificates and police certificates. Staff usually check only the sealed folder and passport, and anything you rely on should already be in CEAC.
  • Plan for 1.5 to 4.5 hours in the building. The interview itself runs 20 to 40 minutes now that officers complete a public charge worksheet. The wait for the interview is the longest part. Bring a book: you get no phone signal inside.
  • Expect public charge questions, asked of you, about your sponsor's income as well as your own. Rehearse them from the public charge page and the question bank.
  • Answer to match your DS-260 and CEAC. Be honest, accurate and conversational. Wear business casual.

4. If approved

The officer tells you on the spot and gives you a domestic violence information pamphlet. Passport is retained for visa printing. You'll receive an email from the embassy with collection instructions.

Typical passport return: Mail Boxes Etc (MBE): 2–3 days for pickup, 5–7 days for mail delivery.

5. If not approved

221(g) with documents ticked: the most common London outcome short of approval. Supply exactly what is ticked on the green sheet, all at once, after the follow-up email. You have one year from the date on the sheet. Tick your boxes and get a to-do list · How to resolve it.

221(g) with administrative processing ticked: a different thing. A required review with no set timeframe, and nobody can speed it up. Often you have nothing to send, but some posts ask for 15 years of history and a CV. What it means · History worksheet.

Refused before, now called back to interview: see re-interview after a 221(g).

Visa pauses: both the nationality visa pause and the diversity visa pause have been struck down, and the State Department rescinded the guidance behind them on 10 September 2026. Cases refused solely under either are being reconsidered. See both visa pauses vacated.

Denial: Rare. Officer explains reason. Limited recourse; consult attorney.
