              What this site is

              American Visa Guide fills the gap between official USCIS guidance, which is thorough but dense, and forum posts, which are specific but hard to trust. It's focused entirely on the US Embassy London consular process for family-based immigrant visas.

              Two things: a community data tracker showing real timelines from real applicants, and a plain-language guide to every stage of the process. Both are built on what people in the community have experienced.

              Independent

              This site is independent and volunteer-run. It is not affiliated with USCIS, the US Department of State, or the US Embassy London.

              This site is most useful to people in a relatively straightforward family-based immigrant visa case applying through London.
