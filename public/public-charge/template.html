The statement template

Updated 23 October 2026. Part of public charge: what you must prove, and how.

Use the builder unless you want to write your own

The statement builder writes this same statement from a form and drops the sentences that do not apply. The templates below suit you if you want to write it yourself. The first serves a spouse, a son or daughter, or a parent, with options marked for each. The second is for a child under 18, written by the petitioner. Replace every bracketed placeholder. Keep each block to one or two paragraphs.

Open the statement builder
Show the full template: spouse, son or daughter, or parent

The blocks run in the order the officer needs them: income answers each cost, and savings come last. Give the petitioner's income both before and after tax; the I-864 uses gross figures and posts' questionnaires ask for net. The appendices list work and education history because posts ask for lists.

Show the template for a child under 18, written by the petitioner

You, the petitioner, write this on the child's behalf and sign it. The officer reads the child's schooling, custody and care, then your income. If the child will become a citizen on arrival under INA 320, say so; otherwise say you signed an I-864. Which applies.
