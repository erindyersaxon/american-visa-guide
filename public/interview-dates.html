Your dates

    How the 6-month medical window works

    Medical results are valid for 6 months from the date of the exam. The immigrant visa is issued to match, so you must enter the US within that same 6-month period. The clock starts at the medical, not the interview.

    • A rescheduled interview uses up part of the window. If the new date is close to the end of it, there may not be time to get the passport back and travel.
    • If the interview falls after the 6 months, the medical has expired and you will need a new one.
    • The medical should be at least 10 working days before the interview, so results reach the embassy.
