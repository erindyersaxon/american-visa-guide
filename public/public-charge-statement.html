Public charge statement builder

Fill in the form and the builder writes your statement in the order the officer reads it: income first, savings last. It works for a spouse, a child, a son or daughter, or a parent of a US citizen. The bar at the bottom of your screen counts the gaps left. It takes about 30 minutes.

Before you start Get the petitioner's figures first: income before and after tax, debts, and insurance details. The officer questions you, not your sponsor, and your answers must match this statement. Boxes marked optional drop out of the statement when you leave them empty. Other empty boxes show as [brackets] until you fill them. Your answers stay in this browser.

7. Savings, assets and debts
Savings back up regular income; they do not replace it. London has refused cases that met the I-864 asset test on paper. Make sure sections 4 and 5 show a salary, pension or joint sponsor that pays the costs, and the statement lists savings last, as a buffer. Why.

Give your own savings, property and debts as the petitioner, and any savings in the child's name under “Held by”.
