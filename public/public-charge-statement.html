Public charge statement builder

Fill in the form and the builder writes your statement in the order the officer reads it: income first, savings last. The bar at the bottom of your screen counts the gaps left. It takes about 30 minutes.

Before you start Get the petitioner's figures first: income before and after tax, debts, and insurance details. The officer questions you, not your sponsor, and your answers must match this statement. Boxes marked optional drop out of the statement when you leave them empty.
